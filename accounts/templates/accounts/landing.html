How it works

Set it up once. Then forget it's there.

Inbox Agent works inside the tools you already use, so there's nothing new to learn.

1

Connect Google

Create an account, then allow access to Gmail and Calendar on a single Google screen.

2

New email gets triaged

Every few minutes, each new message is matched to one of your labels and given an urgency.

3

Your day adjusts itself

Emails are labelled in Gmail, and the ones that need a careful reply get time on your calendar.
